Before 2027 gets here, what is actually holding your brand back?

Hi there,

I have had a lot of the same conversation lately. Business owners who are doing good work, who want next year to look different from this one, and who cannot quite say what needs to change first.

Everything moves faster now. AI has changed how quickly things get made, and the pressure to keep up is real. But the businesses I see getting stuck are rarely stuck because they are slow. They are stuck because they are not sure where the problem actually is. So they fix the thing that is easiest to see, usually the logo, and six months later it is the same problem with a new logo.

After ten years of doing this, I can tell you it is almost never the logo. There are four places a brand gets stuck, and only one of them is a design problem.

I know booking a call with a stranger can feel like a lot.

Completely fair. So I built a mini me. Twelve questions, about three minutes, and she asks far fewer follow-up questions than I do. She will tell you which of the four is holding you back, why it is happening, and the one move to make first. Then you get the full breakdown in your inbox to keep.
